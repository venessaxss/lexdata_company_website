LexData hosted a three-day hybrid workshop titled **"Python for Language Sciences & Social Sciences: Corpus Analysis, Text Mining & Digital Research Workflows"** from 28 February to 2 March 2026 at the NCBA&E Wapda Town Campus, Multan. The workshop was led by **Dr. Muhammad Afzaal**, Associate Professor at the Institute of Language Sciences (ILS), Shanghai International Studies University (SISU), China, together with **Ms. Shanshan Xiao**, Python Expert at ILS, SISU.

The workshop was designed to help researchers and students in the language sciences and social sciences move from manual, small-scale text analysis to reproducible, Python-based computational workflows. Across three days, participants worked through the complete research pipeline — from designing a well-documented text corpus to analysing it, interpreting the results, and reporting findings in a transparent, reproducible way.

## Workshop Outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants were able to:

- Design and document a research-ready text corpus with metadata.
- Perform frequency analysis, concordance (KWIC) and collocation analysis.
- Apply introductory sentiment analysis and topic modeling concepts.
- Compare texts across genres, authors, institutions, ideologies or time periods.
- Export findings into CSV/Excel and write reproducible method notes.
- Integrate computational analysis into theses, articles and funded projects.

## Workshop Format

The workshop ran over three days — **28 February, 1 March and 2 March 2026** — in hybrid mode, allowing on-campus participation at Wapda Town Campus alongside remote attendance. The programme brought together the linguistic expertise of Dr. Afzaal and the Python and programming expertise of Ms. Xiao, giving participants both the research perspective and the technical skills needed to apply computational methods in their own work.

Participants who completed the workshop received e-certificates.
