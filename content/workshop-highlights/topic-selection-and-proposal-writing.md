## About the workshop

The **Two-Day International Workshop on Topic Selection and Proposal Writing** was organised by LexData and held on **4–5 January 2026** as a live online session on Zoom, starting at 4 PM Pakistan Time. It was designed for researchers and students who want to move from a broad interest to a focused, defensible research topic, and from there to a clear and convincing proposal.

The sessions were led by **Dr. Muhammad Afzaal**, Associate Professor at the Institute of Language Studies (ILS), Sichuan International Studies University (SISU), China, and Visiting Professor at University College Dublin (UCD), Ireland.

## What participants worked toward

The workshop was built around five outcomes:

- **Refined research focus:** narrowing a general idea into a specific, workable topic.
- **Proposal structuring expertise:** organising a proposal so that its aims, scope, and plan are easy to follow.
- **Effective literature use:** using existing studies to position and justify a project.
- **Methodological rigor:** choosing and describing methods that fit the research question.
- **Improved academic writing:** presenting ideas with clarity, precision, and a scholarly tone.

## Format

- Two days of live, interactive sessions delivered online via Zoom
- Open to participants from Pakistan and internationally
- E-certificates for participants
