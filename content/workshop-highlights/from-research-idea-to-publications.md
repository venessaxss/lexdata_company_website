LexData hosted a three-week intensive programme titled **"From Research Idea to Publications: Future-Ready Language Research — Corpus, AI, Analytics, and Publication Success."** Running from **27 July to 14 August 2026** (weekdays only), the workshop guided participants through the complete research journey, from shaping an initial idea to preparing a manuscript for publication.

The programme brought together six researchers from universities across Asia and Europe, giving participants direct access to a wide range of methodological and publishing experience in the language sciences.

## Programme Structure

The workshop was organised into three focused weeks, each building on the previous one:

- **Week 1: Research Design, Corpus Building & Dataset Preparation.** Participants learned how to frame a research problem, design a sound study and prepare well-structured corpora and datasets for analysis.
- **Week 2: Python, R, Statistics & Visualization.** The second week moved into the analytical toolkit, covering programming in Python and R, statistical methods and the effective visualization of research findings.
- **Week 3: Academic Writing & Publication Preparation.** The final week focused on turning analysis into a publishable paper, with emphasis on academic writing and preparing manuscripts for submission.

The workshop was delivered by an international panel of experts:

- **Dr. Muhammad Afzaal**, Shanghai International Studies University, China
- **Changpeng Huan**, University of Macau, Macao SAR
- **Ian Joo**, Otaru University of Commerce, Japan
- **Yufeng Liu**, Lancaster University, UK
- **Rafi Ullah Khan**, Liverpool John Moores University, UK
- **Muhammad Faseeh**, Jeju University, South Korea

## Who This Programme Was For

The programme was designed for those working in and around the language sciences, including:

- Master's (MS/MPhil) students in Linguistics, Applied Linguistics, TESOL, Translation Studies and related fields
- PhD scholars seeking advanced research and publication skills
- Early-career researchers planning international publications
- Academics, faculty members and researchers in Language Sciences
- Corpus linguists, discourse analysts, translation researchers, computational linguists and NLP researchers

Participants who completed the workshop received e-certificates.
